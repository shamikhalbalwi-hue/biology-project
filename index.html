Healthier Food for Students

Healthy food helps your body and brain. Here are simple ideas for students.

Eat many colors. Fruits and vegetables give vitamins and fiber. Try to eat some at every meal.

Choose whole grains. Brown rice, oats, and whole-wheat bread give steady energy.

Eat beans, fish, eggs, or nuts. They help muscles grow.

Drink water. Sugary drinks have a lot of sugar and few nutrients. Water is better.

Eat less salt and less fried food. Too much salt can raise blood pressure later in life.

Eat breakfast. It can help you focus in class.

Small changes work. Change one habit this week. Ask a friend to join you.
